# B2 — Parlementaires (députés, sénateurs)

**Status:** sprint 1 (discovery) done: `docs/branches/B2-DISCOVERY.md`, PR #30, decisions of JM in its §6. Sprint 2 (cards for
the current terms, Sénat votes, data gaps) on `b2/cards`, data live on the shared project; earlier terms in progress.

## Mission
Fiches des députés et sénateurs de Martinique : présence, votes, amendements, propositions de loi, questions écrites, par législature.

## Owned paths (only B2 edits them)
- Route: `src/app/parlementaires/**`
- Components: `src/components/parlement/**` · domain logic and reads: `src/lib/parlement/**`
- Strings: `messages/fr/parlement.json`, `messages/gcf/parlement.json` (Kréyòl only where agreed)
- Scripts: `scripts/parlement/**` (`sources.py`, `tables.py`, build scripts) · data: `data/parlement/**`, `data/raw/parlement/` (gitignored)
- Database: tables prefixed `parl_`, migrations `supabase/migrations/<timestamp>_parl_<topic>.sql`

Shared files (card shell, core tables, `common.json`, layout, config, CI…) change only through a separate PR to `dev`.

## Data sources (from docs/BRIEF.md §4)
Assemblée nationale et Sénat (open data), NosDéputés / NosSénateurs (Regards Citoyens), RNE, HATVP. Every source is added to `scripts/parlement/sources.py` and downloaded with
`python -I scripts/core/download_sources.py --domain parlement` (manifest with licence, date, sha256).

## Data model
People and mandates go in the core tables (`institutions`, `bodies`, `seats`, `persons`, `mandates`; `acquired` =
direct_election / indirect_election / appointment). Domain facts go in `parl_*` tables, each row with a `source_id`.

## Card
Built from `src/components/card/Card.tsx` (header with portrait and credit, sourced fact blocks, missing information,
caveats, right of reply, opinion block kept apart). Neutral: no score, no ranking.

  `…/14/loi/amendements_legis_XIV/Amendements_XIV.json.zip` (104 MB, linked from the XIVth archive page
  `archives-anterieures/archives-14e/amendements`; correction of 7 Nov 2026: the 404 was our guessed path, not a gap). **Estimate:** the XVIIth fetchers work on the same fields; the work is the
  one-file layout (XIV) and the two big amendment zips (stream, run alone): about 2 days for votes + amendments +

- **Senate votes (Q4 b), done 7 Nov 2026 without scraping:** the Dosleg PostgreSQL dump (data.senat.fr, Licence
  Ouverte) has `scr` (4 820 public votes since 2006-2007, object, totals) and `votsen` (1.68 M nominative positions with
  status: congé, président de séance, déport…). Cross-checked on senat.fr (scrutins 2026-9 and 2026-48: positions,
  totals and result identical). Gaps found and shown: 13 votes of the term have positions but no `scr` row (e.g.
  2024-41, which senat.fr publishes); no solemn flag; no « n'a pas pris part » count. Dumps carry Windows-1252
  typography as C1 characters: decoded as cp1252 by `_pg_decode` (Ameli and Questions re-downloaded).
- senat.fr legal notice (kept for the legal review): « Les travaux parlementaires ne sont couverts par aucun droit
  d'auteur »; reproduction allowed if free of charge, unaltered and citing www.senat.fr. Nothing about automated
  extraction; no open licence named. Scrutin pages would be HTML to parse (one page per scrutin). To confirm in the
  legal review before any scraper. Note: the same notice says other content (photographs…) is under copyright, which
  concerns the senators' official portraits (JM's decision Q6: credited, removed on request; legal review).

  meetings carry `participants.participantsInternes` with `presence` = présent / absent / excusé, but **only 3 581 of

  JM, 7 Nov 2026: keep it hidden. Stated as a data gap (`an-17-presence-commission`: 3 559 of 5 872 confirmed meetings
